Background

A bit about
who I am

I'm Mustafa Khan — a Computer Science student at Sacramento State with a genuine obsession for how things work, whether that's a piece of software or a turbocharged engine. I got into tech through PC building, which taught me more about troubleshooting and systems thinking than any classroom.

My experience spans web development, QA, and hands-on hardware. Most recently I worked as a VR Technical Support specialist at Fever's Titanic VR experience in Sacramento — running daily QA checklists on headsets and workstations, doing live root-cause analysis on malfunctions, and keeping everything running smoothly under pressure in front of a live audience.

I care about precision in everything I build. That same attention to detail that makes me good at QA and debugging is what drives me toward motorsport — where fractions of a second and marginal gains are everything. Currently building out my dev portfolio with AI-powered tools and theme design while finishing my degree.
